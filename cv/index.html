Professional & Research Experience

Google — Machine Learning Engineer — YouTube Shorts
September 2025 – Present · Mountain View, CA, USA
  • Generative Sequential User Modeling (TokenMinds): Co-authored and deployed an LLM-adapted encoder-decoder user model (370M MoE encoder / 370M dense decoder in JAX) unifying short-form, long-form, and search histories via hierarchical RQ-VAE Semantic IDs; serves billions of users asynchronously (1.44M read QPS, 96.4% cache hit rate), cutting upstream training and serving compute by 50% and 31% while driving statistically significant live A/B gains on YouTube Shorts (+0.13% Satisfied Engagement, +0.11% Engaged Users, +0.33% Freshness Engagement).
  • Long-Context Compression & Representation Stability: Extended sequential user modeling to long interaction horizons using soft-token compression, sparse cross-attention bottlenecks, and multi-resolution recency compression. Established longitudinal representation-stability diagnostics (cosine drift, perturbation invariance, split-history self-retrieval, and effective-rank monitoring) and non-autoregressive auxiliary reconstruction objectives to prevent representation collapse under continuous daily training.
  • Causal Point-Process Objectives, Variance Reduction & Cold-Start Estimation: Designed multi-task sequence objectives for noisy implicit-feedback streams, incorporating right-censored survival/hazard modeling, count-process supervision, deterministic importance/loss weighting, and C++ streaming input filters with strict request-time causality checks against lookahead label leakage; developed empirical-Bayes shrinkage representations for cold-start candidate scoring.
  • Optimization Symmetries & Low-Precision Training Numerics: Investigated orthogonal gauge symmetries and outlier-mitigation techniques for low-precision (FP8/INT4) quantization-aware training under matrix-sign and adaptive optimizers; profiled end-to-end XLA/TPU HBM, VMEM, and operator-fusion rooflines on large-scale multi-task ranking architectures.
  • Scaling Laws & Experimentation Infrastructure: Conducted Iso-FLOPS capacity, batch-size scaling, Maximal Update Parameterization (μP), and low-compute proxy transfer studies to predict full-scale ranking performance; led multi-launch model integration cycles and built automated training-lifecycle governance and paired statistical evaluation tooling for continuous TPU training pipelines.
