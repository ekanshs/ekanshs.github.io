Machine Learning Engineer · Google (YouTube Shorts)

Ekansh Sharma

Ph.D. in Computer Science, University of Toronto

I am a Machine Learning Engineer at Google working on large-scale recommender systems for YouTube Shorts. I completed my Ph.D. in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Toronto, where I was advised by Daniel M. Roy and affiliated with the Vector Institute. I also hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science and a B.A.Sc. with Honors in Electrical Engineering from the University of Toronto.

My work spans machine learning methodology, optimization geometry, and statistical inference. At Google, I build LLM-adapted generative sequential user models, long-context compression bottlenecks, low-precision (FP8/INT4) quantization-aware training pipelines, and scaling-law methodology in JAX/XLA. During my Ph.D., I investigated the geometry of neural network loss landscapes—studying linear mode connectivity modulo permutation symmetries, non-local model merging, and sparse optimization dynamics—to design parsimonious, compute-conscious learning algorithms.

Earlier in my research, I studied probabilistic programming, Bayesian nonparametrics, and heavy-tailed random graph estimation, and spent a term as an Applied Scientist Intern on Sponsored Display Ads at Amazon.
